# The Door Problem: a sacred human mystery

78 seconds · 1280 × 720 · H.264 / AAC · 16:9

Forty seconds (51.3%) are MiniMax H3 segments generated through the ai& API. The remaining footage is the actual Godot demo and two graphic cards. Main narration uses ElevenLabs Aimee, a refined Australian female voice. Only the von Neumann quote uses ElevenLabs George, a male voice (eleven_multilingual_v2). Music is an original synthesized pizzicato bed.

The satire targets human exceptionalism about programming, not the claim that game design takes work. Liz England’s original essay explains the latter.

**Picture:** A human professor treats an ordinary door as a sacred museum artifact.

**Narration:** Behold: the door. Apparently, the final frontier of artificial intelligence. A wooden rectangle, protecting the sacred mystery of being human.
